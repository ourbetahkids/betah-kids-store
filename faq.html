Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: November 2026

Quick answers to the questions customers ask us most. If yours is not here, message us on WhatsApp.

Getting started

Do I need an account to shop?

No. There is no sign-up and no password. Just add items to your cart and fill in your name, phone number, email and delivery address at checkout.

How do I shop on this website?
  • Find what you want using the menu or the search bar.
  • Open the product and choose the quantity.
  • Tap Add to Cart, then open your cart and go to checkout.
  • Fill in your details and choose how you want to pay.
  • We contact you on WhatsApp, by phone or by email to confirm your order.
Are my cart items saved for later?

Yes, on the same phone or computer. Your cart is kept in your browser until you place your order or clear your browser data. It will not appear on a different device.

What if an item is sold out?

Sold-out items are marked and cannot be added to the cart. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you if it is coming back or suggest something similar.

Payment

How do I pay?

You can pay by bank transfer, using the account details shown at checkout, or choose Pay on Delivery if you are in Abuja. After a bank transfer, please send your payment proof on WhatsApp so we can confirm your order quickly.

Does it matter if I pay by transfer or on delivery?

Pay on Delivery is available in Abuja only. If you are outside Abuja, please pay by bank transfer. Your order is confirmed only after we contact you.

Should I pay into any other account?

No. Pay only into the account details shown at checkout on this website. If you are not sure, confirm with us by WhatsApp or phone before you send any money.

Can I pay by card online?

Not at the moment. We accept bank transfer, and Pay on Delivery in Abuja.

Orders

How do I check the status of my order?

Message us on WhatsApp or call us, and give the name and phone number you used when you ordered. We will tell you where your order is.

How will I know my order has been sent?

We update you by WhatsApp, phone or email when your order is on its way.

Can I get a receipt or my order details?

Yes. Ask us on WhatsApp or by email and we will send your order details.

Delivery

How long does delivery take, and what does it cost?

We deliver across Abuja and nationwide. Time and cost depend on where you are, so please confirm both with us on WhatsApp before you pay.

Can I collect my order from the shop?

Yes. Our shop is at No. 41T IS, Shop 457, Wuse Market, Abuja. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and on Sunday by appointment only.

Will everything come in one package?

When we confirm your order, we will tell you if your items will come in more than one package.

What should I do when my order arrives?

Check your items straight away. If something is damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, tell us the same day on WhatsApp or by phone, with a photo if you can.

Cancellations and returns

How do I cancel an order?

Message us on WhatsApp or call us as soon as you can, before your order is dispatched. If you have already paid and your order has not been dispatched, we will refund you.

Can I return an item if I change my mind?

Yes, within 7 days, if the item is unused with its tags and you have proof of purchase. We offer an exchange first. If no exchange is possible, we refund you by bank transfer. See our Refund and Returns Policy.

What if my item is damaged or faulty?

Tell us the same day. We will replace it or refund you at no extra cost to you.

Are there items that cannot be returned?

Yes: underwear and socks, opened pacifiers, feeding and bath items, and items marked final sale or sold at clearance prices. The full list is in our Refund and Returns Policy.

How do I return an item?

In Abuja, bring it to our shop with your proof of purchase. Outside Abuja, message us on WhatsApp first and we will tell you how to send it back. The full steps are in our Refund and Returns Policy.
