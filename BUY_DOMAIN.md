BUY DOMAIN NAME

WHERE: Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Google Domains
SEARCH: betahkids.com (or client's preferred name)
PRICE: About 10-15 USD per year

CONNECT TO YOUR SITE:
1. Buy the domain
2. Go to Vercel dashboard → Domains → Add domain
3. Enter your domain name
4. Vercel shows you settings to add (usually 2 records: A and CNAME)
5. Add those records in Namecheap/GoDaddy
6. Wait 5-30 minutes
7. Your site works at the new domain

NOTE: Your site stays the same. Only the web address changes.
