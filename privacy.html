Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Betah Kids Beauty World LTD (“Betah Kids”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website and in our dealings with you, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

1. Who we are

We are a children’s fashion shop at Block 43, Shop 459, Wuse Modern Market, FCT Abuja, Nigeria (Head Office), with a Branch Office at #92, Trademore Avenue, Phase 2 Trademore Estate, Lugbe, FCT Abuja, Nigeria. We decide how the information described on this page is used.

2. Information we collect

  • When you place an order: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and city, the items you ordered, the total, and the payment status.
  • When you contact us by the contact page, WhatsApp, phone or email: your name, your contact details and the message you send.
  • Payment proof: if you pay by bank transfer and send us proof of payment, we see what you send us.
  • Basic technical details: like every website, our website and the services that run it receive details such as your IP address and browser type when you visit.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask for your card number on this website, because you pay by bank transfer or on delivery. We do not ask you to create an account or a password.

4. How we use your information

  • To confirm your order, arrange delivery or collection, and keep you updated.
  • To answer your questions and deal with returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To keep business, accounting and legal records.
  • To keep the website safe and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Who else handles your information

  • Our database service: your orders are stored in an online database provided by Supabase.
  • Our website host: this website is hosted by Cloudflare.
  • Google: the fonts on this website come from Google Fonts, and the contact page shows a Google map. Google may receive technical details such as your IP address when these load.
  • Delivery: a rider or courier who delivers your order will get your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • WhatsApp, banks and email providers: if you contact us or pay through these, they handle your information under their own rules.
  • The law: we may share information if a court, the police or another authority lawfully requires it.

6. Your shopping cart

The items in your cart are saved in your own browser, on your phone or computer. They are not sent to us until you place an order. Clearing your browser data removes your cart.

7. Cookies

You do not need an account to use this website. We do not knowingly use advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services such as Google Maps may set their own cookies when they load on the contact page.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep order and contact records for as long as we need them to deal with your order, returns and questions, and to meet our accounting and legal duties. After that we delete them or make them anonymous.

9. Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including storing orders in a secure online database and serving this website over HTTPS. No system on the internet is completely safe, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also object to how we use it, or withdraw consent you gave us. To do any of these, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm who you are first, and we may have to keep some records that the law requires us to keep.

11. Children

This website is for parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The latest version is always here, with its date at the top.
