Refund and Returns Policy

Last updated: November 2026

We want you and your little one to be happy with your purchase. If something is not right, we are here to help. Please read this policy before you return an item.

1. Return window

You can ask to return an item within 7 days of the day you received it or bought it in the shop. We cannot accept return requests after 7 days.

2. Conditions for a return

  • The item must be unused, unworn and unwashed, in the same condition you received it.
  • All original tags, labels and packaging must still be attached.
  • You must show proof of purchase: your order confirmation, order number, payment proof or receipt.

3. Items we cannot take back

For hygiene and safety reasons, and because of how they are sold, we cannot accept returns of:

  • Underwear and socks.
  • Pacifiers, feeding items and bath items that have been opened or removed from their sealed packaging.
  • Items marked as “final sale”, or sold at clearance prices.
  • Personalised or custom-made items.

The exception is an item that arrives damaged, faulty or wrong. See section 4.

4. Wrong, damaged or faulty items

If your item arrives damaged, faulty, or is not what you ordered, please tell us the same day by WhatsApp, phone or email, with a photo if you can. If your item is being delivered by a dispatcher, please do not accept it until you have checked it. We will replace the item, or refund you, at no extra cost to you.

5. Change of mind

If you simply change your mind about an item that is in good condition and meets the conditions in section 2, we will offer you an exchange for another item of the same value or a different one (you pay any difference). If no suitable exchange is available, we will refund you by bank transfer.

Delivery fees are not refundable.

6. Refunds

Once we have received and checked your returned item, we will tell you that your return is approved. We will also tell you how long your refund will take. Refunds are paid by bank transfer to an account you give us, in the name of the person who ordered.

7. How to return an item

  • In Abuja: bring the item, with your proof of purchase, to our shop at Shop 457, Wuse Market, during opening hours.
  • Outside Abuja: message us on WhatsApp first. We will tell you how to send the item back and who pays for the return delivery. Please use a trackable delivery service and keep your receipt, because we cannot be responsible for items lost on the way back to us.
